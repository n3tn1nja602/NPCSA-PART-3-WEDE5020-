Our Services

Supporting animal welfare through protection, education and awareness.

How Animal Welfare Is Supported

Animal welfare work includes responding to concerns about cruelty, promoting responsible animal care and helping the public understand the needs of animals.

Animal Cruelty Prevention

Concerns about suspected animal cruelty can be reported so they can be referred to the appropriate animal welfare organisation for assessment.

Farm Animal Welfare

Farm animals need suitable food, clean water, shelter and handling that reduces unnecessary stress and suffering.

Animal Care Awareness

Public education can help people understand animal needs and make more responsible choices.

Responsible Pet Ownership

Pets need regular care, appropriate shelter, safe surroundings and attention to their health and wellbeing.

Need to Report an Animal Welfare Concern?

If an animal appears to be in danger or neglected, gather accurate information and contact the appropriate SPCA or animal welfare authority. Do not put yourself in danger.
