About the NSPCA

Working towards a safer and kinder world for animals.

Who We Are

The National Council of SPCAs (NSPCA) is an animal welfare organisation in South Africa. Its work focuses on protecting animals from cruelty, abuse and neglect.

Animal welfare depends on responsible people, public awareness and action when animals are being mistreated. The NSPCA works to promote better treatment and protection for animals.

Our Mission

Our mission is to prevent animal cruelty and promote the responsible treatment of animals through education, investigation and animal welfare work.

Our Values

Compassion

Animals deserve kindness, care and respect.

Responsibility

People must provide animals with proper care and protection.

Protection

Animal cruelty and neglect should be reported and addressed.

Every Animal Matters

From companion animals to farm animals, all animals need suitable food, water, shelter and protection from suffering. Communities can help by treating animals responsibly and reporting suspected cruelty.
