Pet Care

Responsible Pet Ownership

Pets depend on people for food, clean water, shelter, exercise and appropriate veterinary care. Responsible ownership also includes keeping animals safe.

Animal Safety

Creating a Safe Environment for Cats

Cats need access to clean water, suitable food, comfortable resting areas and a safe environment where they can express natural behaviours.

Understanding Farm Animal Needs

Farm animals need appropriate nutrition, clean water, shelter and careful handling to help protect their health and wellbeing.
