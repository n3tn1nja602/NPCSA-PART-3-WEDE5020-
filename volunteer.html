Become a Volunteer

Give your time to support a kinder world for animals.

How Can You Help?

Volunteering is one way to contribute to animal welfare. Available opportunities depend on the needs, rules and resources of each organisation or local SPCA.

Animal Care Support

Ask local animal welfare organisations whether they need assistance with approved animal care activities.

Community Awareness

Help share information about responsible pet ownership, animal welfare and reporting suspected cruelty.

Fundraising

Support approved fundraising activities to help organisations continue their welfare work.

Volunteer Interest Form

Complete this sample form to show your interest. It does not send your information to the NSPCA; contact the organisation directly to ask about real opportunities.
